# LIMEN Round 8 — Decision-Ready Research Brief

Consolidated from 13 scout steal-row clusters (~230 individual game-mechanic steals) and 5 fresh-eyes persona critiques (first-time player, competitive grinder, streamer, senior systems designer, structural/doc-integrity critic). Source: round8 scout JSON dump, cross-checked against `GAME_DESIGN.md`, `DESIGN_ROUND_7.md`, `BLOCKERS.md`, `core/config.js`, `core/game.js`.

Operator-requested levers: **roads · traps · on-place-effects · instants/sorceries · action-stages · economy**.
Limen problem tags used throughout: **slow-open · no-interaction · no-push-pull · shallow-placement · no-big-turns · no-wow**.

## (b) Consolidated structural critique (deduped across 5 personas)

### BLOCKER

1. **The opening 6-10 plies are geometrically guaranteed to have zero player interaction.** `CAPITAL_MIN_DIST_FROM_SEAM=2` on `GRID_H=9` forces median first-capture to T9-10 regardless of bot aggression (BOT_RIFT_NUDGE swept 3/5/8/12 with zero effect — `BLOCKERS.md`). The team's own Gate-2 fresh-eyes judge independently confirmed this as unresolved. First-time-player testing quit at turn 6 before ever seeing a capture. **Fix is board geometry** (steals #2, #5, #6, #14), not tuning — BLOCKERS.md already says so.
2. **Single-placement-per-turn caps the branching factor low enough that depth-2 search crushes the trained policy** (policy* loses to search2 98-99% of games). Combined with adjacency-gated everything, turns are structurally shallow — a hallmark of a near-solved abstract game. Fixed by steals #4, #8, #18, or a scoped version of the AGoT-style action-stage runner-up.
3. **No per-turn resource/cost differential.** Every card in hand is equally playable the instant drawn; rationing happens only at deckbuild time (copy caps), never at play time. This deletes the entire sequencing/opportunity-cost axis the design doc claims to have ("MTG meta, no resources" — `GAME_DESIGN.md:10`). The doc's own pre-authorized fallback (a flat per-tile cooldown, *not* a bankable currency) has been sitting un-triggered despite its stated trigger condition ("if playtesting demands it") being met twice over.
4. **Deckbuilding has no synergy/archetype axis.** `validateDeck()` only checks total size and per-rarity copy caps — nothing gates composition by tag, faction, or curve, so legal decks converge on "best 2 rares + best N commons by raw power" rather than distinct identities.
5. **The "MTG meta on a hex board" pitch is not what's shipped.** No stack, no instant-speed action, no live blocking decision — rites are sorcery-speed only and combat is deterministic math off fully-visible state. Either re-pitch honestly as a Go/Stratego/Hive-lineage majority-capture duel, or ship one real narrow instant-speed action (steal #18) before building more content on the sorcery-only chassis.
6. **A bankable in-match "big-turn economy" contradicts an already-litigated, dated design decision.** `GAME_DESIGN.md`'s own "Stripped" and "Pacing valve" sections removed resources/costs by name and pre-authorized only a flat per-tile cooldown as the sanctioned fallback — not a player-tracked currency. No document names "turns feel too uniform" as an observed problem. Treat any economy lever (steals #6, #9, #10, #16) as requiring the same evidence bar, and keep it scoped to the pre-authorized shape.

### MAJOR

7. **Capital placement — the literal first action of the game — carries almost no decision weight.** Legal region is broad, gated only by row-band + neighbor-count, with zero preview of consequence or opponent-position signal.
8. **THE RIFT STIRS, the one scripted spectacle beat, fires too late (ply 12) and never escalates** (`RIFT_STIRS_ESCALATION=0`) — a bored player has typically quit before it lands once, and repeat viewers learn by the 3rd-4th occurrence that the VFX is a bluff.
9. **R3 high-ground pressure is flat-capped at `HIGH_CAP=2`** regardless of actual height differential, directly undercutting the marketed "stack higher, harder to combat" fantasy the moment a tower is 2+ taller than a neighbor.
10. **~70% of games end via silent influence-tally subtraction, not a decisive action**, and the biggest moment in the game (capital-capture KO, the other 30%) shares its code path with a routine tile capture — no distinct fanfare, freeze-frame, or escalated beat.
11. **No genuine comeback/swing lever exists** — every scoring magnitude in `config.js` is small and capped (SIEGE_BONUS=5 flat, TIER_BONUS capped at 3, RIFTLIGHT capped at 8), and the Drama Index's own comeback-rate metric has never been reported.
12. **Hand secrecy is soft-hidden only** — deterministic given seed+decks, exchanged client-side, derivable via devtools in an afternoon. Documented as an accepted v1 leak but must be a hard blocker before any ranked/competitive mode.
13. **The original decision-thinness diagnostic ("only 6-15% of legal moves are meaningfully distinct") was never re-measured after RULES-7 shipped.** The team is inferring the disease is cured from downstream proxy metrics (Drama Index, fun score), not by closing the loop on the metric that originally found the problem.
14. **Faction identity (Verdant vs. Umbral) is purely cosmetic.** No faction field exists anywhere in the tile pool or deck-composition code; both players draw from the literal same card pool with the same starter composition. The narrative promise of "each player IS a reality" is unbuilt.
15. **`GAME_DESIGN.md` self-contradicts on rift passability** — a stale "Rift" section (dated one day before RULES-7) still says rift hexes are impassable, directly contradicting the core-loop section and the shipped code (which has no rift-blocking check at all). Real regression risk: a future session trusting the stale section could silently break the Riftlight mechanic.
16. **The proposed Roads and Traps levers are architecturally the same shape as two exploits already patched once** (SCOUT's turn-1 capital rush, closed via `SCOUT_HEARTLAND_BAN`; the pre-RULES-7 "capturable from anywhere" hole, closed via adjacency-gating in R2). Any reach-extending or ambush mechanic must ship with equivalent gating from day one and clear a dedicated adversarial bot arm, not just a mirror-winrate check.
17. **The P1/P2 tempo correction (`INFLUENCE_TIEBREAK_BONUS_P1=-1`) is a flat, invisible fudge applied only inside the influence-resolution path**, calibrated solely against greedy-bot mirror matches (never against search2/policy* or by win-condition split), leaving open whether capital-KO games are quietly skewed while influence-decision games are overcorrected to compensate.

### MINOR

18. Closing `SCOUT_HEARTLAND_BAN` removed the one early-game reach tool with no compensating early-game hook added.
19. `ASCEND_VARIANT='A'` shipped without a cited sim number, unlike every sibling config knob in the same file.
20. DOUBLESTRIKE and TRAMPLE are near-miss spectacle mechanics: DOUBLESTRIKE (a real 2x burst multiplier) is absent from both design docs' highlight lists, and TRAMPLE's splash damage never finishes off a second capture even when the target is already at ≤0 influence.
21. All balance acceptance numbers (Drama Index, win-bands, tiebreak) were calibrated only against a 1-ply "greedy" reference bot, not the team's own stronger bots (search2, policy*) — understating how solved the current ruleset may be against a competent opponent.
22. Sorcery-speed rites already exist and are underused by count (10% starter density), not absent — before building true instant-speed (a large structural rewrite), try raising rite density first.
23. On-placement-effect plumbing (`KEYWORD_HOOKS`) is already proven and cheap to extend (SUSTAIN/TRAMPLE/TIDEBOUND shipped without touching `game.js` core) — this is flagged as the *lowest-risk* of all six levers and should be sequenced first, not last.

## (c) Design tensions

**Roads vs. simplicity.** Printed edge-segments, chain-adjacency legality, and forked path-validation expand the tile data model and rule surface on a currently minimal, fast-to-simulate core. Directly risks reopening the "reach bypasses adjacency" exploit class that `SCOUT_HEARTLAND_BAN` and R2 already had to close once — any road/reach mechanic needs the same heartland-style gating and its own adversarial "road-rush" bot arm before shipping, per the structural critique's own analogy to SCOUT.

**Action-economy vs. bookkeeping.** The entire pacing model (`DECK_SIZE=20`, capture-rate targets, median-ply ceiling) was sim-gated specifically at `PLACEMENTS_PER_TURN=1`. Any action-stage or multi-placement-per-turn lever (Dominion chaining, Polytopia spend pools, AGoT order-stage resolution) invalidates that gate outright and requires a full A7-A10-style resim, not incremental tuning — scope the first version narrow (one chain-trigger keyword on a handful of tiles, steal #4) rather than a global turn restructure.

**Trap tiles + instants vs. a clean deterministic sim.** Limen's best-tested, highest-praised shipped feature is V3's live placement-preview (exact influence math shown before commit) sitting on top of a fully deterministic, fully legible board. Face-down traps and instant-speed response windows reintroduce genuine hidden information and priority-passing that both directly undercut V3's promise and require rewriting the strict turn-alternation model and the multiplayer action-log relay protocol (`player !== currentPlayer` is hard-gated everywhere in `game.js`). Scope traps as "hidden only until placed, fully visible to both players the instant it lands" to preserve V3's legibility, and scope instants as one narrow, bounded response beat (Spirit Island's 2-bucket Fast/Slow model) rather than a full MTG stack.

**Big turns vs. the 25-45 ply target.** The shipped pacing gate (deck size, capture rate, Drama Index) was calibrated to hold games inside a specific ply band. A resource-banking/ramp economy or Storm-style scaling payoff risks blowing that band either direction: bomb turns that end games early undermine the deliberately-tuned midgame RULES-7 just delivered, while banking-to-ramp play (holding back to build toward a payoff) risks dragging games long and reintroducing the exact "no big swingy turns because nothing compounds" flatness the stripped-resources decision was originally meant to avoid. Any economy lever must re-run the full median-ply/zero-capture/win-band sim battery before being accepted, exactly like R1-R9 were — not shipped as an incremental content add.
