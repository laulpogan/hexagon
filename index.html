LIMEN

a hex-tile duel across the threshold

How to play

GOAL

  • Capture the enemy capital ♛ — or, when both sides run out of cards, hold more total influence on the board.

YOUR TURN — one action

  • Place a card on an empty hex touching one of your tiles (Scout cards ignore this).
  • Capture: any enemy tile showing 0 (red ring) is in revolt — place a card onto it from anywhere, no adjacency needed. The old tile is destroyed.
  • Ascend: place onto your own tile to build a tower.
  • Cast a rite ✦: select the rite card, then click a highlighted target.
  • Extras: right-click a card once per turn to discard + redraw. Pass is allowed only when you have no legal move.

INFLUENCE — the number on every tile

  • Shown number = base + each adjacent friendly tile's influence − each adjacent enemy tile's influence, minus rift and ruins. Strong neighbors matter more than many neighbors.
  • Hover any tile or card for its full breakdown and reminder text.

RUINS — the land remembers

  • Every destroyed tile scars its hex. Standing on scars costs −1 per layer (max −2). Fought-over ground grows worthless — recapture for position, not profit.

TOWERS

  • Stack up to 3 high on your own non-capital tile: +1 influence per buried tier, but only the top card's keywords count.
  • Capturing a 2+ tower only peels its top layer — your card bounces back to hand and the hex scars. Deep claims fall one turn at a time.

THE RIFT ◆

  • The glowing seam is impassable and drains −1 per adjacent rift hex. Rift-Attuned tiles gain +1 there instead and ignore ruins.

KEYWORDS

DECK

  • 20 cards: max 2 rares, max 6 uncommons. Build yours in the Deck Builder.

Multiplayer

Play a friend over the rift. Custom decks apply.
