LIMEN

a hex-tile duel across the threshold

How to play

  • Goal: capture the enemy capital ♛, or hold more total influence when all tiles are spent.
  • Each turn: draw 1, place 1 tile next to your own tiles. Right-click a card to discard and redraw (once per turn).
  • Influence: a tile's number is its base plus friendly neighbors, minus enemy neighbors. At 0 it is in revolt: the enemy can place onto it and take the hex.
  • The rift ◆: the glowing seam drains 1 influence from every adjacent tile. Rift-attuned rares feed on it instead.
  • Keywords: hover any card badge. Ward blocks one capture, Rally boosts neighbors, Siege drains enemies, Scout ignores adjacency, Fortified holds edges, Double Strike counts twice.
  • Deck: 20 tiles, max 2 rares, max 6 uncommons. Build yours in the Deck Builder.
