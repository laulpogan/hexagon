## The cosmology in one page

Two whole realities, each complete, each lawful, each blind to the other.

**The Verdant** perfected life into permanence. Its forests are glass and
gold. Nothing rots there; growth keeps everything it makes. A Verdant city is
a single tree grown patient for ten thousand years. Its people did not defeat
death; they out-remembered it.

**The Umbral** perfected transformation. Appetite is its physics. Its cities
burn what they were to become what they will be, lit forever by their own
spending. Nothing in the Umbral is wasted, and nothing is kept. Its people do
not mourn; they kindle.

Neither is evil. Both are whole. The catastrophe is not that one was wrong —
it is that both are true.

No one knows which side broke the law. The two realities intersected along a
line. Where they met, both failed: **the Rift** — a seam where nothing stays
true, leaking raw unreality. The luminous debris of that leak are the
**motes**: sparks of possibility that never chose a world.

Around the wound formed **Limen**, the threshold country. Ground here can
belong to either world — but it must end up belonging to one.

## How war works in a place where nothing dies

Reality in Limen is contested by **conviction**. Each player IS a reality.
The cards are **Aspects** — a thicket, a wolf, a saint — truths a world can
write into the threshold. Placement is inscription: *here, my world is true.*

Nothing fights. Nothing dies. When ground stops believing in an Aspect, the
Aspect is **unwritten**. Conquest does not erase — it **buries**: the
conqueror's truth is written over the old one, and the old truth survives
underneath, dormant, pressed into the ground like a fossil argument. Ground
fought over again and again rises — layer on layer of contradicted conviction
— until the battlefield itself towers. Scholars call these **war strata**.
Soldiers call them what they are: high ground. A deep truth presses down on
its neighbors; attacking uphill means arguing against every buried layer at
once.

Rubble gathers where truths were unwritten — shattered crystal, dead embers,
timber of threshold-folk works that stopped being believed. The land keeps
receipts.

And the seam? The ground nearest the Rift is the ground that decides. The
wound never healed; it **listens**. Whichever reality holds the land beside
the Rift when the war exhausts itself is the reality the threshold chooses.
That is why every war in Limen collapses toward its center. That is why the
most dangerous ground is the most valuable. Every sixth turn, the wound
breathes — **the Rift stirs** — and everything beside it is tested.

Each world enters Limen through one anchor. The Verdant's is the
**palace-tree**, a capital grown from a single seed of the home forest. The
Umbral's is the **ember spire**, a tower of banked fire that remembers the
home city. Unwrite an anchor and its whole world drains out of the threshold.
If both anchors stand when the armies are spent, the threshold itself
decides: whichever reality holds more ground — weighted by the listening
seam — becomes true.

## The Threshold Cycle — three acts

**Act I — The Meeting.** Before Limen there was no "between." The realities
drifted, each infinite, each alone. Then the line. The Verdant felt it as a
cold that memory could not keep out. The Umbral felt it as a fire that gave
no light. Where they touched, both laws failed at once; the Rift tore, the
motes bled, and around the wound a country condensed that belonged to
neither — hexes of possible ground, waiting to be told what they were.

**Act II — The Inscription War.** Both worlds sent what they could spare:
not armies — arguments. Aspects, inscribed one per heartbeat by each
reality's whole attention. First the scouts and lanterns, going ahead of
belief. Then walls, altars, wolves, saints. The first time a Verdant thicket
was unwritten by an ember-runner, both worlds understood the stakes: this is
not a border war. Only one of us leaves this place real. The ground began to
stack. Chokepoints rose into towers of buried conviction. The threshold-folk
— the people of the between, who had lived quietly at the crossing before
either world noticed — built palisades and wardstones and sold their masonry
to both sides, because when only one world survives, you want your work
remembered in it.

**Act III — The Listening Ground.** The war has no end date, only an
arithmetic. Every campaign converges on the seam, because the seam decides.
Veterans of the strata say you can hear it: stand on rift-adjacent ground
and the wound hums with your own conviction, doubled. The Rift stirs on its
slow heartbeat, testing whoever camps beside it. Somewhere ahead is the last
inscription — the day a palace-tree gutters out or an ember spire goes cold,
or the day both stand exhausted and the threshold, patient as ground, simply
chooses. The players of Limen are living that day, one game at a time. Every
match IS the myth: two realities, one threshold, and ground that believes
the last thing said with enough conviction.

## Codices

### Codex Viridis — the Verdant
Aspect-truths of permanence: the THICKET that keeps growing, the LANTERN
seed that goes ahead, the ALTAR that reminds the ground, the HERALD's
certainty, the MIRRORSAINT in whom every believer sees themselves, the
GALEHARRIER above the strata. The palace-tree anchors them. Verdant war
doctrine: keep everything, forget nothing, grow until the question of
victory answers itself.

### Codex Cineris — the Umbral
Aspect-truths of transformation: the OUTCROP fist, the SKIRMISHER and REAVER
who make ground doubt, the FANGWOLF pack, the LEECHSPRITE appetite, the
JUGGERNAUT momentum, the DREADMAW no lone conviction dares contradict. The
ember spire anchors them. Umbral war doctrine: spend everything, waste
nothing, become whatever wins.

### Codex Liminis — the between
What the wound made and what the threshold-folk built: ECHO (a thing that
happens twice), VEILWISP (a flicker rites cannot find), RIFTWALKER and
RIFTWARDEN (fed by the seam that drains all others), and the neutral
masonry — PALISADE, WARDSTONE, BASTION, COLOSSUS — sold to both sides.
Rift-born Aspects invert the wound's tax; the threshold-folk's works simply
refuse, once, to stop being believed.

### The Rites
Not weapons — petitions. A reality leaning in for one heartbeat. SUNDER:
your world briefly refuses to acknowledge a place exists. FORESIGHT: your
world shows you what it wants to become. RALLYING CRY: one heartbeat of
surging belief, kept forever.
